GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What is Studio HASH iNf Lk™?+

An independent creative studio connecting graphics, technology, photography and videography, and music creation under one main brand.

How do I request a project?+

Use the project enquiry form or contact us by email or WhatsApp with a short description of your requirements. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking or order.

Which services are available now?+

iNfpirex is available for graphic design enquiries. iNfysona is considering a limited number of music and audio projects. iNfyzora is in development, and iNframex is not yet open for bookings.

Can I download software from this website?+

When a product is officially released, its page should show the version, supported platforms, requirements and verified download links. Unreleased products will not have active download buttons.

Can I pay online?+

This version does not process online payments. Project scope, pricing, availability and payment arrangements are discussed directly before an order is confirmed.

Are enquiries or reviews saved on this website?+

Not yet. The current forms prepare an email in your default email application. Firebase persistence and secure admin review tools are a separate integration phase.
